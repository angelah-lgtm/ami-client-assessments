    The standard

    Lead the account before the client has to ask.

    Three times in every engagement, the Account Manager steps back from the weekly numbers, assesses the account as a whole, reviews it with leadership, presents it to the client in a strategy session and puts the changes in motion. Client Success then follows up on the progress and results.

    Weeks 3, 6 and 9 About 15 minutes each Every account, every time

    Proactive retains

    Clients who hear about a fix from us stay. Clients who have to ask for one start evaluating alternatives.

    Clear ownership

    Strategy sits with the Account Manager. The client experience sits with Client Success. Each team does its best work in its own lane.

    Growth starts at week 3

    Renewals and expansions are earned early. A sharp week 3 is what makes the week 10 conversation easy.

    Module 4

    The assessment: four areas, every time

    Start with an overall status, assess the same four areas, and close with the plan. The structure never changes, so leadership and Client Success always know where to look and you never start from a blank page.

    Overall status

    GreenYellowRed plus one sentence on why
    1

    Rep performance

    Assess every rep on the account individually.

    • Activity and quality against expectations
    • Communicating with the client directly
    • Doing the meeting handoff and intro
    • Following up to confirm meetings
    • Your call: keep, reset or replace
    2

    Data and KPIs

    The actual numbers, and what they're telling you.

    • Are people picking up? Connect rate
    • Connect to conversation to meeting conversion
    • Which titles and industries are responding
    • MQLs, SQLs, meetings booked and held
    • Our KPIs vs. the contract and the client's expectations
    3

    ICP and strategy

    Should we pivot, and why?

    • Is the ICP right, or is the data pointing elsewhere?
    • Other industries the client should explore
    • Services in their offering that aren't in the Business Profile
    • Anything that could lift results or open an expansion
    4

    What's working, what's not

    The honest read, in a few lines.

    • What to double down on
    • What to stop or change
    • Then the plan: one or two changes, each with a date
    • And what Client Success needs going in: client mood, expectation gaps
    Count what's real

    A meeting that no-showed or an SQL that never booked is not a result. Report what would hold up if the client audited it.

    How to gather it (recommended, use what works for you)

    ClientSide

    Numbers, connect rates and pace for the date range.

    Call recordings

    Listen to recent connected conversations. Which titles and industries are engaging?

    Last 3 weeks of Fireflies

    Client calls. What has the client asked for, and was it delivered?

    Internal and client chats

    Are reps communicating with the client, doing handoffs and confirming meetings?

    Module 5

    The checkpoints

    Same four areas, different focus. Each checkpoint has one question to answer and one decision to make.

    Is the foundation right?
    FeedsWeek 4 Client Success check-in

    Look at

    • List quality and title fit
    • Messaging and opener
    • Each rep's performance and client communication
    • Client responsiveness and approvals

    Decide

    What needs to change now. If a rep isn't performing, week 3 is where the rep reset process starts.

    Client Success uses it to

    Confirm the client feels heard and that expectations match what we can deliver.

    The rep reset process

    Starts at week 3 for any rep who isn't meeting expectations. Everyone knows where they stand. Nothing is a surprise.

    Step 1

    Call with the rep

    Talk through what you're seeing in the data and on the calls.

    Step 2

    Reset expectations

    Be specific: activity, quality, client communication.

    Step 3

    Follow-up email

    Put the expectations in writing with a date 7 days out. CC the Head of Operations and Delivery.

    Step 4

    Review on the date

    Check the results against what was agreed.

    Met

    The rep stays. Check in again in 2 weeks.

    Partially met

    Case by case, decided with the Head of Operations and Delivery.

    Not met

    Replace the rep.

    Are the changes working, and are we on pace?
    FeedsWeek 8 Client Success check-in

    Look at

    • Impact of the week 3 changes
    • Pace against the contract
    • Outcome of any rep resets
    • New ICP or industry signals in the data

    Decide

    If behind, a recovery plan with week-by-week steps and a clear target. Any open rep reset should be resolved by now.

    Client Success uses it to

    Speak to the plan the Account Manager already shared and how it's performing.

    Will this client renew or expand, and what's the proof?
    FeedsWeek 10 Client Success check-in

    Look at

    • Meetings held and pipeline created
    • Client feedback on lead quality
    • Where the next term could grow

    Decide

    Likely to renew, at risk, or ending. Pull the two or three strongest proof points and name the expansion opportunity.

    Client Success uses it to

    Lead the renewal and expansion conversation, backed by real results.

    Expansion opportunities to flag

    Add reps

    Results support more coverage.

    Right-size the team

    Fewer reps, better fit for the budget or market.

    New territory

    A new region, industry or segment the data supports.

    T2

    The account is ready for the next tier of service.

    Module 6

    Handoff and escalation

    Your status sets the response. The earlier we know, the more options we have.

    Green

    On track

    Review in your 1-1, present to the client, keep executing.

    Yellow

    Off pace, fixable

    Present the plan with dates. Yellow two checkpoints in a row gets a deeper review in your 1-1.

    Red

    At risk

    Don't wait for the checkpoint. Flag the Head of Operations and Delivery the same day.

    The handoff

    WhenEnd of week 3, 6 and 9 from campaign start. Reminders are set for every account at launch.
    Send itEmail the assessment to Client Success and CC the Head of Operations and Delivery, the day before your weekly 1-1.
    Review itWalk through it in your 1-1 before the client sees it.
    Present itStrategy session on your weekly client call. The Client Success check-in follows up on it, so the client never hears it there first.
    Rep resetsRun the rep reset process and CC the Head of Operations and Delivery on the follow-up email.
